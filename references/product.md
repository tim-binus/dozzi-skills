## Wearing

- Place over the eyes and centre the nose panel on the nose bridge.
- Wrap around the outside of the hair. Hair lies inside the band. Do not bury the band under a curtain of hair to conceal an incorrect side.
- Overlap and fasten at the back, comfortably snug. Show light fabric tension rather than compressing the head.
- A forehead pose is a paused-use pose: mask rests above eyebrows and around hair, the nose insert no longer sits on the nose. Do not force normal eye-wearing alignment onto this pose.
- The historic neck pose was abandoned in favour of a bedside scene; it is not a default. Use only if freshly requested, with realistic drape/scale.
- Side-lying: pillow indents where the head contacts it. Maintain the mask's alignment and soft edge; don't paste a front view onto a rotated head.
